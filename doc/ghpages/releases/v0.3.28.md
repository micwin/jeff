## Highlights

- `jeff vl` now starts and reuses a Jeff-managed local Vaultline daemon on demand.
- Jeff secrets live in the local `jeff` Vaultline store below the XDG data directory.
- The `jeff` store unseal material is kept in Jeff config and passed transiently, not written into Vaultline's store registry.
